Controls

  • B - Focus board
  • Arrow Keys - Navigate board
  • Enter - Play at selected cell
  • Alt+A - Announce score
  • Alt+M - Announce legal moves

Rules

  • Reversi is played on an 8x8 board. Players take turns placing disks of their color.
  • A move is legal if it traps one or more opponent disks in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) between the new disk and another disk of the same color.
  • Trapped disks are flipped to the current player's color.
  • Example: At the start, if Black plays at D3, the White disk at D4 is trapped between D3 and D5, and flips to Black.
  • The game ends when the board is full or neither player can move. The winner has the most disks.
